BTHomeV2 Logger reads data from Bluetooth Low Energy sensors broadcasting unencrypted BTHome v2 advertisements.

It is designed for sensors such as the Xiaomi Mijia LYWSD03MMC running PVVX / ATC MiThermometer firmware configured to use BTHome v2.

Features:

• Scan nearby BTHome v2 BLE sensors
• Select and remember a sensor
• Display temperature and humidity
• Show battery percentage, battery voltage and Bluetooth signal strength
• Save decoded measurements in a local text log
• Display recent readings in an Android home-screen widget
• Work locally without an account, cloud service or Internet connection

The app currently supports unencrypted BTHome v2 advertisements and common temperature, humidity, battery and voltage objects.
