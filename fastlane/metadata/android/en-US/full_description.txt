BTHome Logger reads data from Bluetooth Low Energy sensors broadcasting unencrypted BTHome v1 or BTHome v2 advertisements.

Features:

• Scan nearby unencrypted BTHome v1 and v2 BLE sensors
• Select and remember a sensor
• Display temperature and humidity
• Show battery percentage, battery voltage and Bluetooth signal strength
• Save decoded measurements in a local text log
• Display recent readings in an Android home-screen widget
• Work locally without an account, cloud service or Internet connection

Encrypted BTHome advertisements are intentionally ignored. The app does not request, store, or use encryption keys and does not decrypt sensor data.

The app supports common temperature, humidity, battery and voltage measurement objects.
